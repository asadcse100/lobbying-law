# Lobbying and The Law — React website

Updated multi-page React/Vite website for Lobbying and The Law (Bangladesh) Company Limited.

## New pages

- `/` — Home
- `/about` — About Us, flagship initiatives, comprehensive services, core strengths and Why Choose Us
- `/llca` — LLCA / Lobbying and the Law Center for ADR, including five ADR panels and B2B/P2P/B2G/Bank dispute segments
- `/ll-cure` — LL-CURE / Lobbying and the Law Company Turnover Research Zone Ecosystem
- `/lobbying` — Lobbying information and public-affairs services
- `/gallery` — Existing gallery with the new Services dropdown integrated

## Navigation

Services is now a dropdown containing LLCA, LL-CURE and Lobbying. The About item is a separate page.

## Hosting

The project remains compatible with Vite, Cloudflare Pages and cPanel/Apache. The existing `_redirects` and `.htaccess` support SPA-style direct URL access.

## Notes on ADR panels

The supplied handwritten reference contains member lists for Panels 1–3 only. Panels 4 and 5 are displayed as roster-pending placeholders rather than inventing names. One ambiguous member entry in Panels 2 and 3 is also marked for confirmation.
